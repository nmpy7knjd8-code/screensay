Day 2

Rain in an Oak Forest

Ivan Shishkin

1891 · Oil on canvas

Russian museum collections (Tretyakov / related oak-forest subjects, 1891)

Oak · Landscape painting

Rain moves through an oak forest as a pale veil; the trunks are dark verticals, the ground a wet brown hush. Shishkin, so often the painter of high summer detail, here lets the weather blur his own exactitude. The wood is a room with a leaking roof.

A third Shishkin, quieter than the moonlit pine, more inward than the mast-tree grove still to come. The forest is a place of thought in Russian landscape as in German Romanticism—a nave without a pulpit. The crop will choose a shaft of trunks and rain rather than a wide clearing.
