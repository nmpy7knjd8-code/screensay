Day 3

Procession in the Fog

Ernst Ferdinand Oehme

1828 · Oil on canvas

Galerie Neue Meister, Dresden

Romanticism · Fog

Monks or townsfolk move through a fog so thick the world is almost erased, a procession that might be liturgical or merely lost. Oehme, a Friedrich pupil, makes weather the true church. Figures dissolve at a few paces; the path is a rumor.

This is inward German Romanticism, less famous than the Wanderer and perhaps closer to lived climate. The fog is a spiritual medium, as in Balke and in certain pages of Novalis, where the visible is only a thinning of the hidden. It is a picture of going on without seeing far.
