Day 4

Reflections on the Thames, Westminster

John Atkinson Grimshaw

1880 · Oil on canvas

Museum and gallery collections (Leeds / public-domain reproduction)

Palace of Westminster · Nocturne

Gaslight and a wet pavement turn the Thames at Westminster into a long brass mirror. Grimshaw’s night is industrial and exact: rigging, a clock tower, a sky the color of tarnished metal. The city is not asleep so much as holding its breath between tides.

Among these nocturnes this one is urban rather than alpine, a cousin to Whistler and to Turner’s earlier river moons. The vertical crop will keep a slice of water, lamp, and tower rather than the whole embankment—enough to feel the wet silence. It is modern melancholy without a symbolist costume.
